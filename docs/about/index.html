About

A short introduction, honestly written.

Hi — I’m Aine. I build web experiences that blend motion, clean design and thoughtful interaction, and I care most about the moment where an interface stops feeling like a form and starts feeling like a conversation.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of research and interface design. That’s meant building tools for questioning economic indicators, translating a three-thousand-year-old divination system into something you can actually use in a browser, and a steady stream of smaller experiments that exist purely because I wanted to see if they’d work.

I’m drawn to projects where the visual layer is doing real work — where motion carries meaning rather than decoration, and where a good layout makes an argument legible that a paragraph couldn’t.

Outside of that: noodles, film photography, playlists that are far too specific, and books that make me rearrange something in my head.

Based in Chicago, IL / Shanghai, China / Hong Kong SAR
Currently Studying + building side projects
